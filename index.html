Why we do this

Two hours where nobody is in a hurry.

The desk is where we talk. It's where we get things wrong, strip a model back down, and start over without anyone getting upset about it. Painting is the excuse; the time together is the point.

We believe that time is a gift and we try not to waste it. So we do the work carefully, we teach what we learn, and we give God the credit for the hands that hold the brush.

Questions

Things
people ask

What paints do you use?

Mostly Citadel with some Vallejo and a couple of Pro Acryl bottles we're testing. Every tutorial lists the exact pots we used, plus a cheaper alternative where there is one.

How old is your son, and is this a good hobby for kids?

He started young and it's been great for him — patience, focus, and a reason to sit and talk. Start with cheap models, keep the sessions short, and let them paint it their way.

Do you take commissions?

Not right now. This is our time together, not a shop — and we'd rather keep it that way. If you want something painted, the tutorials will get you further than we would.

What camera and lights do you film with?

Nothing fancy — a phone on an overhead arm and two cheap desk lamps with daylight bulbs. Good light matters far more than a good camera.

Can we paint together on stream?

Yes — that's the whole point of the live sessions. Bring whatever you're working on, ask questions in chat, and we'll work through it at the same pace as you.
