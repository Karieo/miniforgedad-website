Why we do this

Time at the hobby desk.

A shared hobby, miniature projects, and a place to learn together. Browse what we have painted or choose a technique to try next.

We believe that time is a gift and we try not to waste it. So we do the work carefully, we teach what we learn, and we give God the credit for the hands that hold the brush.

Questions

A few useful answers.

Where should I start?

Follow the Start Here page for our story, gallery, and beginner tutorials. You can start with one model and one technique.

Are the tutorials made by Miniforgedad?

The community library credits external hobby creators and links to their original lessons. Our thinning guide is listed separately.

Where can I find your latest posts?

Instagram is @miniforgedad, and the YouTube channel is MiniForgeDad. The website collects photos and guides from the hobby.

How can I ask about a project?

Email miniforgedad@gmail.com, or use the links to the original Instagram posts in the gallery.
