How it works

The Process

Simple, transparent, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Send me what you want painted — model type, quantity, style reference, and deadline. I'll reply within 48 hours with availability and a quote.

  2. 02

    Ship & Deposit

    Send your models and pay a 50% deposit to lock in your slot. I'll confirm receipt and kick off prep work.

  3. 03

    Paint & Preview

    I send progress photos at key stages so you can approve the direction or request adjustments — no nasty surprises at the end.

  4. 04

    Final Payment & Return

    Once you're happy, pay the remaining balance and I'll ship everything back, professionally packaged and insured.
