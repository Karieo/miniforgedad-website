Our story

A dad, a son,
and a hobby desk.

Building and painting Warhammer miniatures. Learning as we go.

That’s Miniforgedad.

This is our father-and-son hobby project: miniature painting, model building, and getting better at both. We’re learners, and the website is a place to collect the work and useful things to try next.

The gallery includes our Imperial Knight and Psychophage photos. The workbench collection brings in photos from Instagram, with links back to the original posts.

Some of the lessons here are our own notes. The community tutorial library links to the hobby creators who made the original guides, with options for beginners and more experienced painters.
